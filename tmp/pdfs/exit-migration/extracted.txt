01 | DOCUMENT CONTROL
Executive policy and readiness
Portability succeeds when the recipient can use and reconcile the data.
Preserve ownership and lawful access: verify the requester and destination, provide the
approved merchant scope, and maintain required retrieval after transactional service ends.
Provide usable records: structured originals, relationships, documents, financial control
totals, schema information and retained provenance are part of the agreed handover.
Avoid duplicate business events: migration must not issue new invoices, charge another
fee, resend customer receipts or replay government submissions unintentionally.
Prove the result: count, hash, map, import and reconcile before acceptance. Download
completion alone is insufficient.
Close in stages: cut over service, settle commercial matters, retain legally required records
and dispose only of eligible copies under recorded authority.
Current implementation position
NUERS provides a scoped streaming NDJSON export with a schema label, table-level record
counts and SHA-256 values. Selected encrypted business fields are made usable. The reviewed
endpoint does not create a complete binary-document archive, a universal destination importer,
or an automatic exit/deletion workflow.
No merchant has been migrated, deactivated or deleted for this document. No production dataset was
exported. The plan is ready for review and adaptation to a named destination; successful real migration is
not claimed.

Export contents and known
limitations
The allowlist is a baseline, not proof of full service portability.
Implemented behavior
Required qualification
Financial and evidence records
Many transaction, invoice, receipt, source, delivery, review, hold
and related audit tables are scoped. Confirm inventory against
every feature used by the merchant.
Usable selected encrypted fields
Controller decrypts customer profiles/history, selected customer
document reasons, top-up references/review notes and internal
outbox documents. Other encrypted fields may remain ciphertext.
Private document metadata
Verification/customer/branch document metadata may be present;
private_path is excluded for selected tables. Binary file contents are
not streamed by this endpoint.
Hash manifest
Table counts and SHA-256 hashes are produced. The manifest is

Database transaction
Streaming occurs inside a database transaction; actual

No incremental contract

tenant-scoped decrypt-and-re-encrypt supplement, with its own inventory and recipient protection.

06 | HANDOVER PACKAGE
NDJSON structure and integrity
validation
Preserve exact source bytes before parsing or transformation.
Line type
Current contract
header
type=header; schema=NUERS-portability-1; merchant_id; exported_at;
descriptive note. exported_at is not an engine commit marker.
record
type=record; table; record object. The row fields depend on the current
database schema and the allowlist.
manifest
type=manifest; tables mapping each table to records and sha256;
manifest_sha256. This is the terminal completion record.
Validation sequence
Save the original byte stream securely. Require a valid header and terminal manifest; reject
truncated, malformed or unexpected trailing content.
For each table, count its record lines and compute SHA-256 over those exact emitted
record-line bytes including the terminating LF. Do not parse and reserialize first.
Compare per-table counts/hashes with the manifest; reject missing, duplicate or unexpected
required datasets after applying the approved scope.
The manifest hash is computed over PHP JSON serialization of the tables object with its
original ordering/encoding. Cross-language verification must reproduce those exact bytes;
ordinary reformatting can change the hash.
Check every decryption_error and hash_verified flag. Validate relationship and financial
control totals independently of byte hashes.
A matching hash shows byte consistency against that manifest. It does not establish who produced it,
whether the source query was complete, or whether the destination interpreted the records correctly. Keep
transport authentication and business reconciliation as separate controls.

Audit lineage and chain continuity
Preserve evidence of origin without disclosing other tenants.
Retain the source export unchanged, the package manifest, each transformation version and
the source-to-destination map. Record import attempts, rejected rows, operator identity and
approvals in a separate migration audit trail. Do not rewrite the original audit events to make
them look native to the target.
Evidence
Preservation requirement
Scoped audit subset
The export selects events by relevant subject IDs. A filtered subset of a
shared hash chain may contain gaps and is not a self-contained
full-chain proof.
Chain context
Preserve existing previous/entry hashes and source sequence/time.
Obtain a scoped attestation or appropriate checkpoint/context through
controlled review where needed.
Wallet ledger
Validate entry ordering, amounts, previous hash, entry hash, balance
and head before import. Preserve the original ledger as read-only
evidence.
Delivery evidence
Retain provider references, request/response hashes, authentic
acknowledgments and uncertainty states. An internal preparation is not
an external acknowledgment.
Import lineage
Record package ID/revision, source key, transformation ID, target key,
import time, result and review.
Proof custody
Record who generated, protected, transferred, received and verified
each package. Retain receipts independently of temporary download
links.
