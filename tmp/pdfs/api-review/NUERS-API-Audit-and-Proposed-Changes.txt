FINDINGS / PRIORITY 1
Tax and data integrity gaps
Review edition | 18 October 2026
A1 - High | Registration, line tax and invoice family can disagree
Reproduced: a VAT seller can calculate and resolve a non_vat_invoice; a Non-VAT seller with vat_exempt lines resolves
to vat_exempt_invoice. non_vat and vat_exempt both accumulate into vat_exempt_sales. There is no separate SSPT
subtotal.
Proposed: use the reviewed seller registration as authoritative; validate every line and select the legal invoice family from
that registration plus line treatments. Preserve separate Non-VAT SSPT and exempt categories where applicable. Never
use a per-key toggle to rewrite registration.
A2 - High | Buyer address and withholding can disappear
Reproduced: customer_address, withholding_tax and tax_basis_reference supplied to version 1 are removed by
validated(). The calculator is called with withholding zero; total_due equals gross_amount. A payload differing only in
these discarded fields has the same canonical hash.
Proposed: a reviewed versioned schema must preserve buyer details, CWT amount/basis, invoice total and cash
amount due separately. Reject unsupported financial fields instead of silently treating the document as complete.
Preserve the original source snapshot and its hash.
Regulatory basis
VAT mixed invoices need separate VATable, VAT, exempt and zero-rated totals [S1-S2]. The official B5 sample is
a Non-VAT mixed invoice, with SSPT and exempt sales [S3]. RR 6-2026 adds separately shown applicable CWT
[S4].

FINDINGS / PRIORITY 1-2
Security and workflow gaps
Review edition | 18 October 2026
A3 - High | Sandbox key can read production status
Reproduced: a sandbox key for the same merchant and branch successfully reads a transaction first accepted with a live
key. Write and wallet endpoints enforce environment, but show() does not. This is an environment boundary issue;
cross-tenant access remained denied.
Proposed: enforce environment on all data paths and persisted resource ownership. Until a truly isolated sandbox exists,
sandbox credentials must not read live transactions. Add tests for same-tenant cross-environment reads.
A4 - High | Credential changes and audit are not atomic
Reproduced: forcing audit() to fail returns 500 after a key has already been inserted. A new active credential remains,
while its one-time secret was not returned. Revoke similarly updates before its audit call (source review). Key creation
also accepts an unregistered five-digit branch.
Proposed: wrap each lifecycle change plus audit in one transaction; validate an eligible registered branch; serialize
creation policy checks. Never return a secret until its audited creation commits.
A5 - High | External capture is coupled to NUERS issuance
Observed: every new API event reserves the next NUERS authorized series number and creates an issued receipt.
Even an active merchant with a valid key cannot ingest without the issuance gate. Existing ERP numbers and historical
imports may conflict with the enforced prefix plus eight-digit format.
Proposed: distinguish an immutable copy of an already-issued external invoice from an invoice being issued by NUERS.
Design the storage, evidence and numbering rules before exposing either mode. Keep existing gates until this review is
approved; do not bypass authority controls.

PROPOSED EXPERIENCE
VAT settings that match the seller
Review edition | 18 October 2026
A6 - Medium | Preview and selection need registration context
Current document selectors list all eight values, and the public preview infers seller VAT registration from the
header tax_type. Add an explicit synthetic seller profile in preview, while production always uses the reviewed
business profile.
Setting Proposed behavior
Seller tax registration Read-only VAT Registered / Non-VAT from Business Profile. A reviewed effective-date history
preserves the classification used by each original.
Default line treatment For VAT sellers: VATable 12%, VAT-Exempt, or Zero-Rated. For Non-VAT sellers: Non-VAT SSPT /
exempt as legally applicable. This is a mapping default, not a tax-registration switch.
Per-product / line override Keep tax category per line. Allow a product default, then evaluate customer, transaction, evidence
and date. Require a maintained legal-basis reference for exempt/zero-rated use.
Mixed invoice VAT seller: split VATable base, VAT, exempt and zero-rated totals. Non-VAT seller: retain Non-VAT
invoice identity and separate SSPT/exempt totals.
Unsupported cases Do not treat a generic discount_pct as a complete senior/PWD or other statutory workflow. Hold
unsupported special-tax cases for a reviewed mapping.
Proposed validation order
Authenticate merchant / branch / environment → load reviewed seller registration → validate source schema and line
categories → verify evidence and totals → resolve document family → apply relevant capture or issuance rules →
atomically persist and debit once.
A7 - Medium | Guide is currently text-only
Replace both guide download actions with a versioned PDF and keep an accessible HTML companion. Publish only
after this draft is reviewed. Include schema version, examples, known limits, change history and source links. The PDF
supplied with this review is not yet installed on the live page.

DESIGN DECISION
Capture, issue and recover safely
Review edition | 18 October 2026
Mode proposed for Purpose Required guardrails
review
External invoice Store the original legal invoice already Preserve issuer/system, exact original number, date, tax
capture issued in the ERP/POS. snapshot and linkage. Do not reserve a second legal number
or label a second original as issued. Verify source authority
appropriate to that system.
NUERS invoice NUERS generates the legal original. Maintain reviewed authority, branch, system scope and

Offline replacement / Recover an authorized manual invoice, Use a distinct linked document and workflow; retain the
correction or correct an issued one. original. Define separate replacement and correction
semantics, rather than modifying accepted JSON.
Why this requires review
The current contract represents finalized events, yet implements a new issuance. A mode change affects legal
numbering, immutable source identity, fees, receipts, sales reports and BIR readiness. It must be designed with
migrations and backward compatibility, rather than patched with an activation bypass.
Current BIR alignment checkpoint
RMC 98-2026 separates electronic invoicing from sales reporting; PTI and PTT have distinct triggers. Its downtime
provision calls for authorized manual invoices and linked electronic replacements on restoration. It also specifies five
mandatory certification tests, or seven with API callback. A hard-coded blanket seven-stage gate needs mapping to the
actual applicable process [S5].
Recommended release boundary
Ship tax, environment and audit-atomicity fixes together with a reviewed capture/issuance contract. Keep
callbacks and official BIR transmission visibly unavailable until their own verified implementations are ready. Do
not present a funded wallet or active business as BIR authorization.

introduce automatic tax-status changes, production test sales, account activation or webhook delivery as side effects of
deployment.

Source interpretation
The proposed model is an engineering interpretation of the cited rules and current source, not a BIR-approved API
schema. Ordinary 12% arithmetic is covered here; product-specific exemptions, zero-rating evidence, special incentives
and effective dates require maintained transaction-specific rules. The June 2025 advisory and RMC 66-2025 are
relevant qualification sources, not a basis for blanket zero-rating.
