## Requirements applied to development

- Accreditation applies to the named solution, version, interfaces, modules, hosting, and approved deployment configuration. Demonstration and certification environments must remain distinguishable from production. Credentials or a successful local test do not establish accreditation.
- Taxpayer PTI authority, written authorization, EIS certification, and PTT are separate requirements. CAS PTU/AC alone is insufficient. Existing production gates remain enforced.
- Slides 11 and 14 require real or near-real-time transmission within three calendar days of the transaction. Outages require preserved queues, reconciliation, and retransmission within three days of restoration. Original deadlines remain visible separately from recovery deadlines; retries cannot silently reset them.
- Seven stage gates cover documentary review, institutional/financial capacity, architecture/data flow, privacy/security, demonstration, interoperability/transmission, and operational/load/recovery validation. Evaluation evidence must be stored, rather than fabricated from UI status.
- Capacity requirements include five million annual transactions for each of the preceding three years, at least 1,000 active taxpayers per year, and a three-year track record. These are documentary thresholds, not claims that NUERS currently meets them.
- Accreditation validity is five years, with annual review and renewal at least 60 days before expiry. Critical incidents require reporting within 24 hours. Monitoring must preserve evidence and time-stamped audit history.
- Taxpayers retain record ownership. The ESP acquires no proprietary right in BIR tax-administration data. No unrelated advertising, credit scoring, profiling, or sale of taxpayer data; subprocessors require appropriate disclosure, authority, and contractual controls.
- Slide 28 requires standard summary reports within one working day and complete machine-readable transaction extracts within three working days, or a shorter authorized urgent period. Preserve records through the statutory period **and thereafter until written BIR confirmation of successful migration and authorization to discontinue storage**. Termination, fee disputes, provider changes, and the five-year subscription storage offer must not override legal holds or these obligations.
- Transparent fees and practical exit/portability are required. The confirmed pricing rule remains free below ₱500 and ₱2/₱3 per transaction at ₱500+, depending on plan.
