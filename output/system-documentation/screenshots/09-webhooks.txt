  - heading "Receiver Requirements" [level=2]
  - paragraph:
    - text: Use a separate endpoint and signing secret per environment. Verify
    - code: X-NUERS-Signature
    - text: using HMAC-SHA256 over
    - code: timestamp + "." + event_id + "." + raw_body
    - text: . Allow at most five minutes of timestamp drift, compare signatures in constant time, and durably deduplicate the event ID before returning 2xx. Delivery order is not guaranteed.
  - paragraph:
    - text: Verification events use
    - code: webhook.endpoint_challenge
    - text: . After verifying the signature, respond with
    - code: "{\"nuers_challenge\":\"the data.challenge value\"}"
    - text: . Verification leaves the endpoint paused until you explicitly enable it.
  - paragraph: Callbacks contain NUERS record IDs and status metadata. Read the original transaction through your authenticated status endpoint. No API secret, buyer details, invoice image or BIR acceptance is sent in the callback.
  - paragraph: Automatic retries use exponential backoff, up to 12 attempts within 24 hours. Redirects and permanent receiver errors stop retries. A delivery can be manually retried up to three times; the original ID/body stays unchanged. Pause or rotate before maintenance; already in-flight requests may still arrive.
