NUERS invoice numbering and receipt fields — release audit

Scope: Align invoice identity and printed document details with the reviewed BIR requirements while preserving the existing receipt design. Existing source intake remains available. No BIR credentials, outbound transmission or authority approvals were invented or enabled.

Implemented
- Original seller document number preserved, including prefix/leading zeros; EIS CompInvoiceId limited to 50 characters and kept separate from EisUniqueId.
- Server-allocated native invoice series, transaction locks, range and overlap checks, and reuse protection; issued original numbers remain unchanged.
- Registration evidence snapshot and applicable PTI/PTU/AC/date/series/machine details. Missing source evidence is displayed as Not recorded.
- Registered seller TIN plus branch, registered buyer identity, original invoice references, item units/tax treatment, VAT classifications, withholding details/net payable and supplementary-document warnings.
- Same shared receipt renderer and existing design for preview/PDF; print-only spacing adjusted to avoid an orphan footer. Existing receipt stylesheet unchanged.
- Maintained OpenAPI, batch schema, API catalogue, written reference and 48-page merchant guide aligned.
