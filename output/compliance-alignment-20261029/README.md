## Operational boundaries

ESP accreditation enforcement for new source capture remains **disabled as explicitly requested**; existing intake remains available. MFA and tenant boundaries stay enforced. BIR outbound and callback gates remain disabled. The service-invoice issuer is not configured, so official NUERS fee invoice issuance remains blocked pending the registered entity and its authentic authority/series.

BIR accreditation/credentials/supervised certification, independent security certifications, confirmed residency and offsite backup/production DR evidence remain external prerequisites. Passing software tests is not a claim of BIR approval or 100% regulatory compliance.
