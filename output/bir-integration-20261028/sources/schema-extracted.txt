 | F14: Depending on the date the error or duplication was discovered, it has to do with whether to file an amended tax return.

- The date of issuance of the corrected documents should be the same as the issuance date of the document at the time of the actual sales transaction, not the date of finding any errors or duplicates. 
Therefore, if the date of discovery of the error or duplication is in the same month/quarter as the issuance date of the original sales transaction, all corrected documents are included in the monthly/quarterly tax return. There is no need to amend  monthly/quarterly tax return.

- However, if the date of discovery of errors or duplication is in a different month/quarter from the issuance date of the original sales transactions, then any revised documents will not be included in the monthly/quarterly tax return.
In other words, any corrected documents will not be included in the monthly/quarterly report, as the error or duplication was discovered after the monthly/quarterly report.
Therefore, it is necessary to amend the monthly/quarterly tax return.
