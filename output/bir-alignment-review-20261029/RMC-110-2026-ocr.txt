I. PURPOSE
This Circular is issued to provide policies and specific requirements for the accreditation
and regulation of electronic invoicing solutions of ESPs supporting electronic invoicing (e-
invoicing) and electronic sales reporting requirements under Sections 237 and 237-A of the
National Internal Revenue Code of 1997 (Tax Code), as amended by Republic Act (RA) No.
10963 (TRAIN Law), and RA No. 12066 (CREATE MORE Act), and as implemented by
Revenue Regulations (RR) No.8-2022, RR No. 11-2025, as amended by RR No. 26-2025, and
Revenue Memorandum Circular (RMC) No. 98-2026.
Further, this Circular prescribes the institutional, legal, technical, operational, security,
data governance, and continuing compliance requirements for the accreditation and supervision
of e-invoicing solutions. It aims to ensure that the e-invoicing solutions of ESP are reliable,
interoperable, secure, auditable, financially and operationally sustainable, and capable of
protecting taxpayer information while providing the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) complete,
timely, and lawful access to electronic invoice (e-invoice) and sales data required for tax
administration.
II. OBJECTIVES
This Circular is issued to:
a. Establish a clear, transparent, risk-based, and stage-gated accreditation framework
that requires documentary, institutional, technical, security, interoperability,
continuity, and production-readiness validation;
.Ensure accurate, complete, secure, timely, traceable, and tamper-evident generation,
processing, storage, and transmission of invoice and sales data in accordance with
BIR-prescribed performance, availability, integrity, and security standards:

III. COVERAGE
This Circular applies to e-invoicing solutions of ESPs providing e-invoicing, electronic
sales reporting, integration, storage, validation, middleware, and related services to taxpayers.
An internally developed system, or software licensed to a taxpayer and operated on the
taxpayer's own infrastructure, used exclusively by one taxpayer and its registered branches shall
not be covered by this Circular and shall remain governed by existing Computerized Accounting
System (CAS), Point-of-Sale (POS), or other applicable registration rules. A system or service
offered to an affiliate, subsidiary, related party, franchisee, customer, or any other separate
juridical entity, shall be treated as an ESP service unless exempted by the BIR. A provider may
request exemption in writing at the concerned Revenue District Office (RDO) or Large
Taxpayers Service (LTS) Office, and the Bureau shall act on the request within twenty (20)
working days.
IV. DEFINITION OF TERMS
For purposes of this Circular, the following terminologies shall be described as follows:
a. Electronic Invoicing Service Provider -refers to a juridical entity organized or duly
licensed to do business in the Philippines that provides e-invoicing, sales reporting.
integration, validation, transmission, storage, preservation, retrieval, reconciliation,
migration, and audit-support services to taxpayers.
The ESP shall maintain an authorized representative and accountable technical,
compliance, data protection, and security officers in the Philippines.
b. Electronic Invoicing Solution - refers to a system that generates, validates, records,
preserves, and issues e-invoices in a structured electronic format and maintains the
corresponding invoice data and audit trail. It shall also transmit such e-invoices to
buyers/customers and sales data to the BIR.

V. POLICIES AND GUIDELINES
A. GENERAL POLICIES
1. MANDATORY ACCREDITATION. - No ESP shall commercially deploy,
activate, or provide e-invoicing solutions and electronic sales reporting services to
taxpayers unless its solution is accredited by the Bureau and it holds a valid Electronic
Invoicing Solutions Accreditation Certificate. An applicant may conduct product
demonstrations and controlled pre-production testing, provided that it clearly
discloses that the service is not yet authorized for production use and does not process
live taxpayer transactions. Accreditation shall be solution-specific and shall apply
only to the particular solution, version, modules, interfaces and deployment
configuration evaluated and approved by the BIR. Updates that are not classified as
material changes do not require reaccreditation.
2. SCOPE OF SERVICES. - The onboarding of an ESP shall cover all activities
necessary to ensure that the ESP is technically capable, secure, and legally compliant.
An accredited ESP shall provide an end-to-end service supporting the issuance of e-
invoice and electronic sales reporting in accordance with Sections 237 and 237-A of
the Tax Code, applicable revenue issuances, technical specifications, and BIR
requirements.

Such service shall include the generation, issuance, preservation, validation,

The ESP shall:
a. Provide an e-invoieing system capable of generating and preserving structured e-

b. Ensure that the "PTI Electronic Invoice Number" is properly reflected on e-

c. Facilitate the application for, issuance of, or updating of the client-taxpayer's PTI
Electronic Invoice through the BIR-prescribed procedure. The client-taxpayer
shall remain the holder of such registration or authority, and the ESP shall acquire
no proprietary interest in or control over the same.
E-invoice issuance and electronic sales data transmission are distinct but related
compliance functions under the EIS framework. Depending on applicable laws,
regulations, taxpayer classification, and implementation phases, a client-taxpayer
may be subject to e-invoice issuance requirements, electronic sales data
transmission requirements, or both. Coverage under the EIS framework, and the
corresponding services required from the ESP, shall be determined by the
taxpayer's obligations under the Tax Code and applicable BIR issuances.
d. Transmission of e-invoice and sales data shall be made real time or near real time
within three (3) calendar days from the date of transaction. Where immediate
transmission is unavailable, the system shall securely queue, preserve, reconcile,
and retransmit the data within three (3) calendar days upon system restoration
without loss, alteration, corruption, or duplication; and
e. Manage all required Application Programming Interface (API) integrations,
certifications, and pre-production testing on behalf of the client-taxpayer, subject
to the client's written authorization.
3. LEGAL PERSONALITY OF AN ESP. - An applicant may be a corporation,
partnership, joint venture, or consortium organized or duly licensed to do business in
the Philippines.

4. COST TO CLIENT-TAXPAYERS. - ESP fees shall be transparent, reasonable,
proportionate to the services provided, and fully disclosed before taxpayer
onboarding. No ESP shall impose hidden charges, exit, data-retrieval, or migration

providers or retrieving its records. This is a condition of accreditation.
The BIR shall require disclosure of standard packages, fees, material price changes,
and charges imposed for onboarding, storage, transmission, retrieval, migration, and
termination.

under this Circular shall not replace taxpayer-level CAS, POS, or other system

the PTI Electronic Invoice and EIS Certification of the taxpayer under RMC
No. 98-2026.

C. TRACK RECORD AND OPERATIONAL CAPABILITY REQUIREMENTS
1. The ESP shall demonstrate independently verifiable experience, financial capacity,
qualified personnel, infrastructure, scalability, reliability, and operational maturity
appropriate to its proposed service model and projected taxpayer and transaction
volumes.
2. The e-invoicing
solutions shall pass BIR-supervised stress, load, concurrency,
scalability, failover, recovery, and peak-volume tests based on projected and
contingency transaction volumes.
3. The ESP shall maintain a secure, resilient, and scalable cloud infrastructure that
supports high-volume operations, business continuity, regulatory compliance, and
reliable integration with BIR systems. It shall provide documented architecture,
monitoring and reporting capabilities, operational visibility, and full disclosure of
hosting, support, disaster recovery, and administrative access arrangements.
4. The ESP shall demonstrate that, for each of the immediately preceding three (3)
years, it has successfully operated or supported systems collectively capable of
processing at least Five Million (5,000,000) transactions annually and supporting at
least One Thousand (1,000) active user or customer accounts per year.

VII. OBLIGATIONS OF ESPs
The ESP who secured an Electronic Invoicing Solutions Accreditation Certificate shall:
1. Validate the legal existence of the taxpayer-clients before acceptance and onboarding.
Such validation shall include verification of the taxpayer's registration with the BIR and,
where applicable, the appropriate government registering authority.
2. Issue e-invoices for subscription fees and other service-related charges billed to its
onboarded clients and shall transmit the related sales data to the BIR EIS. Such
transmission shall be limited to the ESP's own billings and shall exclude transactions
between the onboarded clients and their respective customers.
3. Comply continuously with all accreditation, technical, security, privacy, interoperability,
performance, continuity, and service requirements.
4. Ensure the accuracy, completeness, integrity, authenticity, non-duplication, sequencing,
and traceability of data processed or transmitted through their systems.
5. Maintain immutable and time-synchronized audit logs accessible to the BIR.

VIII. DATA OWNERSHIP, CUSTODY, STORAGE, ACCESS, AND PORTABILITY
1. Ownership and Control - The taxpayer shall retain ownership and legal responsibility
for its underlying books, records, invoices, and source business data. All e-invoices and
sales data transmitted to the BIR, or stored by the ESP specifically for future transmission
or use by the BIR, shall constitute official tax-administration records under the custody
and control of the BIR. The ESP shall acquire no ownership, lien, intellectual-property
interest, or other proprietary right over such data.
2. BIR Access - The ESP shall provide the BIR complete, direct, secure, timely, and

Summary and Detailed Reports - Upon lawful request, the ESP shall provide:
a. Standard summary reports in the format prescribed by the BIR within one working

Storage Pending BIR Readiness - The ESP shall securely retain complete transaction-
level e-invoices and sales data, associated metadata, acknowledgments, error records,
and audit trails for the period required under the Tax Code and thereafter until the BIR
confirms in writing that the required data have been completely and successfully
migrated to the BIR infrastructure and authorizes discontinuance of storage.
5. Continuing Obligation - Expiration, suspension, revocation, termination of a taxpayer
contract, insolvency, cessation of business, merger, or change in control shall not
extinguish the ESP's storage, access, preservation, confidentiality, audit-support, or
migration obligations.
6. No Vendor Lock-In - The ESP shall not withhold, disable, delete, degrade, or condition
access to data because of a contractual dispute, unpaid service fee, termination, or change
of provider.
7. Portability and Migration - The ESP shall maintain the capability to export and transfer
all relevant data, metadata, logs, and audit trails to the taxpayer, the BIR, or another
accredited ESP without loss, alteration, duplication, unreasonable delay, or unreasonable
cost.
8. Permitted Use - The ESP shall use the data only for authorized e-invoicing, reporting,
storage, support, security, reconciliation, and compliance purposes. It shall not sell,
monetize, profile, combine, disclose, or use the data for advertising, credit scoring,
competitive intelligence, or an unrelated purpose.
9. Personnel and Third-party Service Providers' Disclosure - No affiliate, hosting
provider, cloudprovider, subcontractor, or foreign processor may access or process
covered data unless disclosed to and approved by the BIR and contractually bound to
obligations at least equivalent to those imposed on the ESP.
10. Preservation of Records - The ESP shall support legal hold or preservation orders for
records subject to audit, investigation, enforcement, litigation, or fraud review.
