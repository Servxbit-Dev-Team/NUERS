## Slide 4

PURPOSE
Why This Circular Is Issued

Provides policies and specific requirements for the accreditation and regulation of e-invoicing solutions supporting e-invoicing and electronic sales reporting under Sections 237 and 237-A of the Tax Code.

Implements the TRAIN Law (RA 10963) and CREATE MORE Act (RA 12066) through RR No. 11-2025, as amended by RR No. 26-2025.

Ensures ESP solutions are reliable, interoperable, secure, auditable, and financially sustainable while protecting taxpayer information.

## Slide 7

DEFINITIONS
Key Terms Defined


Electronic Invoicing Service Provider (ESP)
A juridical entity organized or duly licensed to do business in the Philippines that provides e-invoicing, sales reporting, integration, validation, transmission, storage, preservation, retrieval, reconciliation, migration, and audit-support services to taxpayers.


Electronic Invoicing Solution 
A system that generates, validates, records, preserves, and issues e-invoices in a structured electronic format and maintains the corresponding invoice data and audit trail. It shall also transmit such e-invoices to buyers/customers and sales data to the BIR

07

Notes: 7

## Slide 8

DEFINITIONS
Key Terms Defined


Electronic Invoicing Solutions Accreditation Certificate 
A certification issued by the BIR confirming that an e-invoicing solutions offered by an ESP, including its system, version, interface, infrastructure, and production environment, has been approved for use in live taxpayer transactions.


Material Change 
refers to a change in control, system architecture, core functionality, data model, interface, hosting or cloud environment, encryption or authentication method, data location, or any other change listed in the Accreditation Certificate that materially affects security, integrity, interoperability, reliability, or BIR access.

07

Notes: 8

## Slide 9

DEFINITIONS
Key Terms Defined


Permit to Issue (PTI) Electronic Invoice 
The authority granted by the BIR authorizing a taxpayer to issue e-invoices that comply with the prescribed              e-invoicing requirements, through a duly registered and/or approved invoicing system.
 
The PTI is distinct from the Permit to Use (PTU) or Acknowledgement Certificate (AC) for CAS, which authorizes the use of such systems but does not by itself constitute authority to issue e-invoices.


Permit to Transmit (PTT) 
The authorization issued by the BIR permitting mandated taxpayers to transmit sales data to the BIR.

## Slide 11

POLICIES & GUIDELINES
General Policies

1
Mandatory Accreditation
No one may commercially deploy e-invoicing or sales-reporting services without a valid Accreditation Certificate. Accreditation is solution-specific — tied to the particular version, modules, interfaces, and configuration approved.

2
Scope of Services
End-to-end service: generation, issuance, preservation, validation, processing, storage, retrieval, audit support, and transmission. Data must be transmitted in real or near-real time within three (3) calendar days.

3
Legal Personality
An applicant may be a corporation, partnership, joint venture, or consortium. Joint-venture or consortium members are jointly and severally liable for compliance.

4
Cost to Taxpayers
Fees must be transparent, reasonable, and fully disclosed. No hidden charges, unreasonable exit or data-retrieval fees, or restrictions preventing provider changes.

## Slide 12

Mandatory Accreditation

10
No ESP shall commercially deploy, activate, or provide e-invoicing solutions and electronic sales reporting services to taxpayers unless its solution is accredited by the Bureau and it holds a valid Electronic Invoicing Solutions Accreditation Certificate. 
An applicant may conduct product demonstrations and controlled pre-production testing, provided that it clearly discloses that the service is not yet authorized for production use and does not process live taxpayer transactions. 
Accreditation shall be solution-specific and shall apply only to the particular solution, version, modules, interfaces and deployment configuration evaluated and approved by the BIR. Updates that are not Material Changes do not require reaccreditation.
GENERAL POLICIES

SCOPE OF SERVICES

10
Provide an e-invoicing system capable of generating and preserving structured e-invoices that comply with applicable BIR functional, data, validation, security, and audit-trail requirements;
Ensure that the PTI Electronic Invoice Number is properly reflected on e-invoices, as may be required under applicable revenue issuances and technical specifications;
Facilitate the application for, issuance of, or updating of the taxpayer's PTI Electronic Invoice through the BIR-prescribed procedure. The client-taxpayer shall remain the holder of such registration or authority, and the ESP shall acquire no proprietary interest in or control over the same.

## Slide 16

COST TO CLIENT-TAXPAYERS

10
ESP fees shall be transparent, reasonable, proportionate to the services provided, and fully disclosed before taxpayer onboarding. No ESP shall impose hidden charges, exit, data-retrieval, or migration fees that are not disclosed in the service agreement or that exceed the ESP’s documented cost, or contractual restrictions that prevent a taxpayer from changing providers or retrieving its records. This is a condition of accreditation.
The BIR shall require disclosure of standard packages, fees, material price changes, and charges imposed for onboarding, storage, transmission, retrieval, migration, and termination.

## Slide 28

DATA GOVERNANCE
Data Ownership, Access & Portability

Ownership & Control
Taxpayers retain ownership of underlying records. All e-invoice and sales data transmitted to or stored for the BIR are official tax-administration records under BIR custody. The ESP acquires no proprietary right.

BIR Access
The ESP shall provide the BIR complete, direct, secure, timely, and continuous access to all e-invoice and sales data, metadata, audit logs, acknowledgments, rejection and error records, and other information lawfully required for tax 

Summary and Detailed Reports 
Standard summary reports in the format prescribed by the BIR within one working day and
Complete transaction-level extracts in a structured, machine-readable, and non-proprietary format within three working days, or within a shorter period when required for an authorized audit, investigation, enforcement action, cybersecurity incident, or system emergency.

Storage Pending BIR Readiness 
The ESP shall securely retain complete transaction-level e-invoice and sales data, associated metadata, acknowledgments, error records, and audit trails for the period required under the Tax Code and thereafter until the BIR confirms in writing that the required data have been completely and successfully migrated to the BIR infrastructure and authorizes discontinuance of storage. 

Continuing Obligation 
Expiration, suspension, revocation, termination of a taxpayer contract, insolvency, cessation of business, merger, or change in control shall not extinguish the ESP’s storage, access, preservation, confidentiality, audit-support, or migration obligations.

## Slide 29

DATA GOVERNANCE
Data Ownership, Access & Portability

No Vendor Lock-In 
The ESP shall not withhold, disable, delete, degrade, or condition access to data because of a contractual dispute, unpaid service fee, termination, or change of provider.

Portability and Migration 
The ESP shall maintain the capability to export and transfer all relevant data, metadata, logs, and audit trails to the taxpayer, the BIR, or another accredited ESP without loss, alteration, duplication, unreasonable delay, or unreasonable cost.

Permitted Use 
The ESP shall use the data only for authorized e-invoicing, reporting, storage, support, security, reconciliation, and compliance purposes. It shall not sell, monetize, profile, combine, disclose, or use the data for advertising, credit scoring, competitive intelligence, or an unrelated purpose.

Personnel and Third-party Service Providers’ Disclosure 
No affiliate, hosting provider, cloud provider, subcontractor, or foreign processor may access or process covered data unless disclosed to and approved by the BIR and contractually bound to obligations at least equivalent to those imposed on the ESP. 

Preservation of Records 
The ESP shall support legal hold or preservation orders for records subject to audit, investigation, enforcement, litigation, or fraud review.
